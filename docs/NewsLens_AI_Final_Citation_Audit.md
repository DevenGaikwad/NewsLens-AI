# NewsLens AI Final Citation and Provenance Audit

## Audit scope

This retained report checks attribution, training-data provenance, artifact claims, and public-facing statements after the Phase 5S synthetic replacement.

## Findings

1. The public training corpus is independently authored, fictional, and identified by an exact archive size, SHA-256, and Git blob.
2. No external article text is quoted, paraphrased, transformed, translated, summarized, or reconstructed in the corpus.
3. ISOT is mentioned only to document exclusion and historical replacement; no ISOT content or derived private artifact is published.
4. Research papers and tutorials are background references, not training inputs or copied implementation text.
5. Third-party packages are named without claiming ownership; their upstream licenses remain controlling.
6. Model-performance statements cite the locked synthetic final test and are accompanied by a non-generalisation warning.
7. Authorship and copyright consistently identify Deven Sachin Gaikwad.

## Conclusion

The public package accurately distinguishes original project work, separately licensed synthetic data, third-party software, and cited research. No statement treats noncommercial or educational status as automatic copyright permission.
