A neighbourhood group says a proposed traffic redesign may reduce congestion near three schools, although the city has not completed its modelling. The draft would create a one-way section, change two bus stops and add a protected crossing. Supporters point to shorter queues observed during a temporary weekend closure. Shop owners are concerned that delivery vehicles could be forced onto smaller streets. The transport department said it will publish a simulation and collision-risk review before councillors vote. The available observations cover only two days and did not include normal weekday school traffic. A six-week public consultation begins next month, and officials may revise the design after receiving comments. Because the evidence is preliminary, claims that the plan will definitely solve or worsen congestion go beyond the information currently available. The proposed plan remains under consultation.
