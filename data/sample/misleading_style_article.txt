Scientists have finally admitted that a common kitchen spice can replace every vaccine and cure almost any disease overnight, according to a shocking message spreading across social media. The message gives no journal name, author, hospital, trial registration or patient count, but says that thousands of doctors are hiding the discovery because it would end modern medicine. It asks readers to forward the claim before unnamed authorities remove it. The post relies on anonymous testimonials and promises guaranteed results for children and adults without mentioning dosage, side effects or interactions. It also links to a store selling an expensive supplement and claims the offer expires within hours. No national health agency has issued the alleged announcement, and the post provides no original research that can be independently checked. Medical treatment decisions should be discussed with qualified professionals and supported by credible clinical evidence.
